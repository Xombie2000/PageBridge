# PageBridge Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026

PageBridge does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

PageBridge reads visible Japanese text on webpages in Safari solely to translate that text into English. Webpage text and translation requests are processed locally on the user’s Mac using Apple’s Translation framework. The developer does not operate a translation server and does not receive webpage contents or translation results.

Translated strings are cached locally on the Mac. Per-site preferences are also stored locally. PageBridge does not include analytics, advertising, telemetry, accounts, or third-party tracking software.

macOS may contact Apple to download Japanese and English language resources when the user initiates the one-time language setup. That activity is governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

PageBridge does not translate text embedded in images, video, or canvas content.

To remove locally stored PageBridge data, uninstall PageBridge and remove its Safari website-extension data in Safari settings.
